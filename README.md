## 📖 What is this Project?
This project is about analyzing raw sales data and converting it into **clear business decisions**. I used pure Python to find out what drives sales, which products make profit, and how a business can grow faster.

> A small analysis that gives big business impact.

## ❓ Key Business Questions Answered

- Which month gives the highest sales?
- Which product category generates maximum profit?
- How does discount affect our revenue?
- Which region is our strongest market?
- Who are our most valuable customers?

## 🎯 KPIs Tracked
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit & Profit Margin %
- Total Orders & Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Best & Worst Selling Products

## 🔬 Analysis Performed

**1. Time Series Analysis:** Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly sales growth
**2. Product Performance:** Category, Sub-Category analysis
**3. Customer Analysis:** Segment-wise, Top 10 Customers by Revenue
**4. Geographical Analysis:** Region & State performance
**5. Profit Analysis:** Deep dive into loss-making orders

## 📊 Visualizations Created
- Revenue Trend Line Chart
- Top 10 Products Bar Chart
- Region-wise Sales Pie / Map Chart
- Discount vs Profit Scatter Plot
- Category Performance Comparison
- Heatmap for Seasonal Sales

## 💡 Top 5 Insights I Found

**1. Technology is King:** 45% of total revenue comes from Technology category.
**2. The Q4 Boom:** October to December alone contributes 35% of yearly sales. Festival season = Golden period.
**3. Discount is Dangerous:** Orders with >25% discount are mostly in loss. We need to control discounting.
**4. West is Best:** West region leads in both sales and profit.
**5. Small Group, Big Value:** Top 10 customers contribute 20% of total revenue - Retention is key.
